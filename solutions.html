FAQ — Solutions

Common questions

How long does a dashboard build take?
A standard dashboard build takes 7–10 business days from when we receive your data. More complex builds or those requiring significant data cleaning may take longer — we'll always give you a clear timeline upfront.
What data do I need to provide?
We work with whatever you have — Excel files, CSV exports, Google Sheets, SQL databases, or Power BI datasets. If your data needs cleaning first, that's included in the build process.
Do you sign NDAs?
Yes. We treat all client data with strict confidentiality and are happy to sign an NDA before any data is shared.
What if I'm not happy with the result?
Every project includes one round of revisions. We won't close a project until you're satisfied with the output. Our goal is a long-term relationship, not a one-off transaction.

Not sure which solution fits?

Message us on WhatsApp — describe your data problem and we'll tell you exactly what you need.
