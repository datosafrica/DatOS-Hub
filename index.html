FAQ

Questions we get asked most

Do I need any prior experience to join?
No. All our programmes are designed to start from scratch. If you can use a computer and have the discipline to show up, you can complete any of our courses. We also have intermediate tracks for those with existing skills.
How are the classes delivered?
All programmes are delivered 100% online via Zoom or Google Meet. You attend live sessions at scheduled times, get recordings if you miss any class, and have access to your instructor and classmates via a group channel throughout.
When does the next batch start?
A new cohort opens every month. The next batch starts . Spots per cohort are limited — message us on WhatsApp to reserve your place before it fills.
Are there payment plans available?
Yes. We offer flexible payment plans for all programmes. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll work out an arrangement that fits your situation. We don't want fees to be the reason you miss out on a programme.
Do you offer corporate training for teams?
Yes. We design and deliver bespoke data analytics programmes for company teams — from 1-day workshops to full multi-week programmes. We've delivered training to teams at NNPC and Shell. Get in touch for a custom proposal.
Will I get a certificate at the end?
Yes. Every student who completes their programme and final project receives a DatOS certificate of completion. You'll also leave with a portfolio of real projects built during the course that you can show to employers.
What makes DatOS different from other training providers?
Three things: our instructor has delivered training to real corporate clients including NNPC and Shell, not just online students. Every programme is built around real business problems and datasets, not toy examples. And we also build real analytics solutions for businesses — so what we teach is what we actually do.
